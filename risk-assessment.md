#Cybersurity risk assessment 
## 1. Purpose 
The purpose of this assessment is to identify an cybersecurity risks within a fictional organization and evaluate the the controls used t reduce those risks.
## 2. CRitical assests
The organization depends on several important assets, including:

- Customer database
- Employee computers
- Internal network
- Web servers
- Firewall
- Email systems
## 3. Potential Threats 
Potential cybersecurity threats include:

- Phishing attacks
- Malware
- Unauthorized access
- Ransomware
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Insider threats

## 4. Vulnerabilities

Potential vulnerabilities include:

- Weak passwords
- Outdated software
- Poor access controls
- Unpatched systems
- Lack of employee security awareness

## 5. Security Controls

Recommended controls include:

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Regular software updates and patching
- Strong access controls
- Employee security awareness training
- Firewalls and network monitoring
- Regular backups
- Incident response procedures

## 6. Risk Reduction
Organizations should regulatory reveiw there assets, vulenerabilities and security controls. High risk issues should be prioritized and addressed first to reduce the likehood and potential impact of a cybersecurity incident.
