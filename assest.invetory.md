# Asset Inventory

An asset inventory helps an organization identify and track the systems, devices, and information that are important to its operations.

| Asset | Type | Owner | Criticality | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Database | Database | IT Department | Critical | Active |
| Web Server | Server | IT Department | High | Active |
| Firewall | Network Device | Security Team | Critical | Active |
| Employee Laptops | Endpoints | IT Department | Medium | Active |
| Email System | Application | IT Department | High | Active |
| Employee Records | Data | Human Resources | High | Active |

## Asset Management Recommendations

- Maintain a centralized inventory of all organizational assets.
- Assign an owner to each asset.
- Record the criticality of each asset.
- Review the inventory regularly.
- Update the inventory when assets are added, modified, or retired.
- Remove retired assets from active inventory records.
